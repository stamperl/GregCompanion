Click Foundry release notes

r1.2.1 Bulk Planning Patch

Gameplay-facing changes for the public build. Share the PDF version as click-foundry-r1.2.1-patch-notes.pdf.

Patch Notes

  • The Bulk Calculator now provides a complete production-planning workflow with item, quality, and quantity targets; recursive favourite recipes; base-material totals; required setup; and bookmarkable plans with amounts.
  • Planner icons now open their matching recipes or raw-resource views, while recipe choices show the selected method, station, variant number, and favourite status.
  • Changing a favourite recipe now immediately recalculates the plan and confirms whether its base materials changed or the selected variant follows the same material route.
  • The calculator is fully scrollable on phones, uses item icons throughout for faster scanning, includes missing intermediates such as Steel Rods, and treats Sticky Resin as a gathered raw resource.
  • Owned fluids remain outside calculator stock checks for now, avoiding misleading shortages while fluid inventory planning is refined.
  • Multiblock tanks now accept and route fluids through configured input and output faces across the formed structure instead of limiting input to one corner.
  • Steam Turbines now fill and display their 32 L Steam buffer correctly, including while their EU buffer is full, and Well and boiler terminal readings and fluid handling have been corrected.
